# Garden description

The AI reads this file before every decision. Copy it to `garden.md` (gitignored, so
your address and yard stay private) and describe your own garden. Day-to-day
observations ("the top bed looks dry") belong in the dashboard's Garden notes instead.

## Location and climate
- Region and climate type, e.g. "coastal, mild summers with morning fog, rain November–March".
- Anything unusual about the site: wind, frost pockets, reflected heat from a wall.

## The planting
- Size, slope and aspect, e.g. "75 ft strip on a steep south-facing slope".
- What's at the edges that runoff could reach (a sidewalk, a neighbor's yard).
- Sun and shade, and what causes the shade.
- The plants and how they like to be watered, e.g. "succulents: deep, infrequent watering; rot is a bigger risk than drought".

## Irrigation
- Valves, lines and emitters: type, count and flow (GPH), and how they were measured.
- Anything that affects how water lands: spray vs drip, emitters on stakes, wind drift.
- Known problems, e.g. "water runs off the slope after about 10 minutes, so use short cycles with soak time".
